# Ideas that won't be done

These ideas have been thought about and turned down. Requests for them are closed with a link to this page, so the tracker stays clear for new ideas. For what *is* planned, see **Coming next** in the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md).

If you think one of these deserves another look because something has changed, open a feature request that says what's different.

## Rackcraft (the mod)

- **Enemy drone raids.** Hostile drones attacking your base in Superb Warfare worlds. They were tried, and they were more annoying than fun in testing and too hard on performance.
- **Fixed-wing aircraft and airships on the Assembly Line.** Ground vehicles (and helicopters, coming next) can be built on the line, but planes and airships stay dealership-only. The way the line builds a vehicle can't support a fixed-wing aircraft, and airships have the same problem. The line is staying as it is, so this won't change.

## Rackcraft: The Modpack

- **Create: Steam 'n' Rails.** Its trains wouldn't connect with Rackcraft's maglev, so it would be a second rail system that doesn't talk to the first.
- **A "Lite" version of the modpack.** There's one published modpack, and it's the full one. A cut-down version would water down what the Rackcraft pack is meant to be, and the lite version that was tested didn't meet that standard.
